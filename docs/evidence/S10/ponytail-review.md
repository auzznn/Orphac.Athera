## Assessment

1. **Minimal Scope & Authoritative Module Boundaries:**
   - Reused existing authoritative module schemas without duplicating entity stores: supplier return headers, lines, and replacement authorizations live under `procurement.*` (`supplier_returns`, `supplier_return_lines`, `replacement_authorizations`, `return_sequences`, `replacement_sequences`), while append-only supplier credit notes live under `ap.*` (`supplier_credits`, `supplier_credit_lines`, `credit_sequences`).
   - Reused `InventoryValuationService`, `TaxDetermination`, `PayableItem`, `ReceiptAccrual`, and `PurchaseOrder` domain invariants rather than duplicating separate valuation math or tax tables.
   - Separated case-authoring, approval (`approval.decide` + `procurement.approve` vs `ap.approve`), and execution (`receiving.manage`, `inventory.manage | receiving.accept`, `ap.post`) along real S02–S09 RBAC permission grants instead of inventing disconnected roles.

2. **Return Matrix (`OD-05` / `OD-07`), Replacement Valuation & Accounting Invariants:**
   - **M1 (`UNVALUED_DOCK_REJECTION`):** Operates exclusively on unvalued Gate 1 dock-rejected quantities (`DockRejectedBaseQuantity`), posting zero GL journals, zero stock movements, and zero GRNI adjustments; optional `REPLACEMENT_ONLY` issues `ReplacementAuthorization` (`CommercialIncrementalUnitPrice = 0`, `AuthorizedInventoryUnitCostBase = ApprovedPoUnitCostBase > 0`) that reserves capacity against unlinked arrivals under `OD-01` and transfers the authorized cost into Gate 2 valued acceptance (`ReceiveLinkedReplacement` -> `GoodsReceipt.ReplacementAuthorizationId`).
   - **M2 (`VALUED_UNBILLED_RETURN`):** Requires open fiscal period (`OPEN`) and four-eyes approval (`OD-02`), posts outbound `PURCHASE_RETURN_OUTBOUND` from unreserved `UNRESTRICTED` or `BLOCKED` stock (`Cr 1300` at MAC guarded by `OD-04` floor), clears open GRNI via `ReceiptAccrualLine.ApplyUnbilledReturn` (`Dr 2110` +/- `5200 PPV`), and reverses accepted PO quantity via `PurchaseOrder.ReverseAcceptedQuantityForReturn` so unreturned PO capacity cannot be double-billed.
   - **M3 (`VALUED_BILLED_RETURN` + `AP_CREDIT_NOTE`):** Enforces unpaid `POSTED` bill, `WithholdingTaxAmount == 0`, open period (`OPEN`), `ap.approve`, and mandatory `TaxReturnNoteNumber` (`Nota Retur`) when `PpnTaxAmount > 0` (`OD-07`). Executes inside PostgreSQL savepoint `sp_execute_supplier_return` with row-level `FOR UPDATE` locking, creating append-only `ap.SupplierCredit` (`SCN-YYYY-NNNNN`), `PayableItem.ApplySupplierCredit` (`Dr 2100`), `TaxDetermination` (`PPN_INPUT_REVERSAL`, `Cr 1410`), outbound stock (`Cr 1300`), and balanced MAC difference (`5200 PPV`), with idempotency keys & transactional rollback on injected subledger fault (`FaultAfterSubledgerBeforeCommit`).
   - **M4–M7 Fail-Closed Guards:** Explicitly rejects `VALUED_BILLED_RETURN` + `REPLACEMENT_ONLY` (`409 od05_billed_replacement_policy_blocked`), partial/paid invoices (`409 od05_paid_bill_return_policy_blocked`), PPh-bearing invoice lines (`409 od07_withholding_return_reversal_blocked`), and `CLOSED`/`LOCKED` periods (`409 period_closed`).

3. **PostgreSQL RLS, Immutability Triggers & UI Race Safety:**
   - All 8 M10 tables enforce `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` scoped by `platform.current_tenant_id()`, least-privilege `athera_app` grants (`SELECT, INSERT` ONLY on append-only `ap.supplier_credits` and `ap.supplier_credit_lines`), `athera_migrator` policies, database immutability triggers (`trg_procurement_supplier_returns_immutability`, `trg_procurement_supplier_return_lines_immutability`, `trg_ap_supplier_credits_immutability`, `trg_ap_supplier_credit_lines_immutability`), and verified `M10 -> M09 -> M10` schema rollback (`S10_PG_02`).
   - UI cleanly keys `ReturnsSheet` by `${tenantId}:${entityId}` and `SupplierReturnDetailSheet` by `supplierReturnId`, suppresses misleading GL entries during policy-blocked previews (`M4`), and renders complete accessible tabs/tabpanel controls without visual or DOM bloat.
