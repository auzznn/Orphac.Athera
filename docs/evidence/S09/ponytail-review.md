## Assessment

1. **Minimal Scope & Authoritative Module Boundaries:**
   - Isolated AP documents, match evaluation logs, price adjustments, payment block controls, and subledger liabilities under schema `ap.*` (`invoice_match_policies`, `supplier_bills`, `supplier_bill_lines`, `invoice_match_evaluations`, `supplier_bill_receipt_matches`, `invoice_price_adjustments`, `payable_items`, `payable_payment_block_events`), paired with append-only `gl.receipt_accrual_clearings` and `tax.tax_determinations` without creating duplicate inventory or PO copies.
   - Enforced `OD-08` fail-closed scope boundary (`409 od08_non_po_bill_not_supported` on `NON_PO_EXPENSE` and `SUPPLIER_ADVANCE`) and deferred AP payment file preparation/execution and bank reconciliation strictly to `S10`.
   - Avoided duplicate valuation math by extending `InventoryValuationService` (`SimulateInvoicePriceAdjustment` / `ApplyInvoicePriceAdjustment` and `PostOperationalIssueAsync`) and sharing `InvoiceThreeWayMatchCalculator` across live preview, submit, approval, and atomic posting.

2. **Three-Way Match, Stock-Coverage Apportionment & Atomicity (`OD-03`, `DEC-P2P-013`, `OD-04`, `OD-07`):**
   - `OD-03` safe defaults keep automatic `PP` / `DQ` tolerances and `BD` write-off limit strictly at `0` (`EXACT_MATCH_OD03_SAFE`, `UNREVIEWED_SAFE_DEFAULT`), blocking public tolerance configuration changes (`409 od03_unreviewed_tolerance_policy_blocked`) pending Product Owner sign-off in `ATH-CR-006`.
   - `InvoiceThreeWayMatchCalculator` evaluates `DQ` (`remainingUnbilledBaseQty` cap), `PP` (unit price difference vs receipt accrual cost requiring `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` four-eyes `ApprovePriceVariance = true` under `OD-02`), and `BD` (`declaredGrossAmount` vs computed gross blocking on non-zero difference).
   - On atomic post inside PostgreSQL savepoint `sp_post_supplier_bill` with deterministic row locking, `DEC-P2P-013` allocates price variance proportionally to `CoveredBaseQuantity = min(BilledBaseQty, PoolOnHandBeforeQty)` into Inventory `1300` (guarded by `OD-04` carrying floor `newCarrying > 0`, returning `409 od04_valuation_floor_breached` on underflow) and the remaining issued share into `5200 PPV`, while clearing `gl.receipt_accruals` / `gl.receipt_accrual_lines`, recording append-only `tax.tax_determinations` (`PPN` input + optional `PPh 23` withholding), posting balanced GL journal `TX-BILL`, creating open `ap.payable_items` (`2100`), and preserving idempotency under concurrent retries.

3. **PostgreSQL RLS, Immutability Triggers & UI Race-Condition Resilience:**
   - All 11 S09 tables enforce `ENABLE` + `FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY` using `platform.current_tenant_id()`, least-privilege `athera_app` grants (`SELECT, INSERT` append-only on `invoice_match_evaluations`, `supplier_bill_receipt_matches`, `invoice_price_adjustments`, `gl.receipt_accrual_clearings`, `tax.tax_determinations`), `athera_migrator` policies, immutability triggers (`trg_ap_supplier_bills_immutability`, `trg_ap_supplier_bill_lines_immutability`) allowing only payment-block status transitions post-posting, and verified `M09 -> M08 -> M09` schema rollback (`S09_PG_02`).
   - Added request-sequence guard `detailReqSeqRef` in `inventory/page.tsx` so rapid item clicks cannot be overwritten by slower initial fetch responses, keyed `SupplierBillDetailSheet` by bill + status to reset four-eyes checkboxes on transitions/row changes, and wired explicit accessible actions and inline validation alerts without adding bloat.
