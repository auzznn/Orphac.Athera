## Agent orchestration
Engineering decisions on Athera are supported by nine Devin agents: a **Chief of Staff** that coordinates eight specialist agents. The agents prepare options, evidence and recommendations; the Product Owner makes the decision. No agent can approve a slice, sign acceptance or authorize production (see [Do not overclaim](#do-not-overclaim)).

| Agent | Supports decisions on |
|---|---|
| **Chief of Staff** | Tracks what is in flight and who is waiting on whom, routes each question to the right specialist, follows it to done and brings the Owner a short decision brief. |
| Product Engineer | Slice design and implementation against the S00–S18 contracts and the architecture ADRs. |
| QA Engineer | Test coverage, real-PostgreSQL integration and E2E runs, failure and hostile-selector cases. |
| Latency Optimizer | API, query and page performance, with measurements before and after a change. |
| Technical Writer | Design notes, evidence packs, handover docs and this README. |
| Security & Tenancy Reviewer | Tenant isolation, RLS, Identity/session and permission checks (invariants 1–3). |
| Finance & Tax Controls Reviewer | Posting, valuation, AP/AR, GL balance and Indonesian tax rules (invariants 4–11). |
| UX Designer | Impeccable UX audits of shell, tables, forms and accessibility. |
| Release & Evidence Auditor | `WORKSTREAM-STATE.md` status honesty and PO sign-off packets prepared, never approved. |

How a decision flows:
1. The Owner raises a question or the Chief of Staff spots one (an open decision, a blocked slice, a review finding).
2. The Chief of Staff hands it to the specialist(s) who own it and tracks the work.
3. Specialists return evidence: code, test logs, measurements or review notes under `docs/`.
4. The Chief of Staff sends the Owner the options, a recommendation and the evidence links.
5. The Owner decides; the decision is recorded under `docs/decisions/` and work continues.

## Do not overclaim
A completed build is not an accepted feature. A passing unit test is not proof of SQL isolation or accounting correctness. Missing tests are BLOCKED, not PASS. No Product Owner approval signature or production authorization may be generated by the agent.
